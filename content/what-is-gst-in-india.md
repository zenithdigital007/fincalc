# What is GST in India? Rates, Calculator, and Examples (2025)

**GST (Goods and Services Tax)** is India's unified indirect tax system that replaced a complex web of central and state taxes on July 1, 2017. Understanding GST is essential for consumers, business owners, and professionals alike.

## What is GST?

GST is a **destination-based, multi-stage, value-added tax** levied on the supply of goods and services across India. It replaced taxes like:
- Central Excise Duty
- Service Tax
- State VAT
- Entertainment Tax
- Octroi

**"One Nation, One Tax"** — GST unified India's fragmented tax system into a single, streamlined structure.

## GST Structure in India

India follows a **dual GST structure** — both the central and state governments levy taxes:

| Tax | Full Form | Levied by | Applicable On |
|-----|-----------|-----------|---------------|
| CGST | Central GST | Central Government | Intra-state supply |
| SGST | State GST | State Government | Intra-state supply |
| IGST | Integrated GST | Central Government | Inter-state supply |
| UTGST | Union Territory GST | UT administration | UT supply |

When you buy something within your state, CGST + SGST = Total GST.
When goods cross state borders, IGST applies.

## GST Slabs in India (2025)

| GST Rate | Examples |
|---------|---------|
| **0%** | Unprocessed food grains, fresh vegetables, milk, eggs, stamps, judicial papers |
| **5%** | Packaged foods (dal, rice, wheat), sugar, tea, coffee, transport services, economy flights |
| **12%** | Processed foods (butter, ghee), computers, mobile phones, business-class flights |
| **18%** | Most services (banking, telecom, insurance), electronics, restaurants (without AC), most goods |
| **28%** | Luxury goods (cars, motorcycles), tobacco, cement, ACs, dishwashers, casinos |

Most services — from restaurant bills to mobile recharge — fall under **18% GST**.

## How to Calculate GST

### Adding GST (Exclusive — price does not include GST)
If the base price is ₹10,000 and GST is 18%:
- GST Amount = ₹10,000 × 18/100 = ₹1,800
- **Total Price = ₹11,800**

### Removing GST (Inclusive — price includes GST)
If you paid ₹11,800 including 18% GST:
- Base Price = ₹11,800 ÷ (1 + 18/100) = ₹11,800 ÷ 1.18 = **₹10,000**
- GST Amount = ₹11,800 − ₹10,000 = **₹1,800**

## Practical GST Examples

### Restaurant Bill
Your restaurant bill shows ₹800.
The restaurant has AC (18% GST):
- GST = ₹800 × 18% = ₹144
- **Total payable = ₹944**

### Mobile Phone
You're buying a phone for ₹25,000 (MRP, inclusive of 12% GST):
- Base price = ₹25,000 ÷ 1.12 = **₹22,321**
- GST = ₹25,000 − ₹22,321 = **₹2,679**

### Freelance Services
You invoice a client ₹50,000 for design work (18% GST, if GST registered):
- GST = ₹50,000 × 18% = ₹9,000
- **Total invoice = ₹59,000**

## GST for Consumers: Key Things to Know

### 1. Always check your GST invoice
A valid GST invoice must include:
- Supplier's GSTIN (15-digit number)
- Invoice number and date
- HSN/SAC code of goods/services
- GST rate and amount (CGST + SGST or IGST)

### 2. GST is built into prices at retail stores
At supermarkets and retail stores, MRP already includes GST. No extra tax is charged at checkout.

### 3. Restaurants must show GST separately
Restaurants must charge GST (5% for non-AC, 18% for AC) and show it separately on the bill. Charging both "service charge" AND GST is legal — but service charge itself is voluntary.

### 4. E-commerce platforms collect GST
When you buy from Amazon, Flipkart, or Myntra, GST is included in the price. The platform collects it on behalf of sellers.

## GST Registration: Who Needs It?

Businesses must register for GST if:
- Annual turnover exceeds **₹40 lakh** (goods) or **₹20 lakh** (services)
- They sell across state borders (inter-state)
- They sell through e-commerce platforms

Freelancers with turnover below ₹20L can choose to register voluntarily (to claim ITC).

## GST and Input Tax Credit (ITC)

One of GST's key features is **Input Tax Credit (ITC)** — businesses can claim back the GST they paid on purchases (inputs) against the GST they collect on sales.

Example: A shoe manufacturer pays 18% GST on leather (input). When they sell shoes with 12% GST, they offset the input GST paid. This eliminates the "tax on tax" (cascading effect) that existed before GST.

## GST Rate Rationalization (2025 Updates)

The GST Council regularly revises rates. Notable 2024-25 changes:
- Health insurance premiums: Reduced from 18% to 12% (proposed, under review)
- Term insurance: 18% GST (industry seeking reduction)
- Electric vehicles: 5% GST (down from 12%)

Always use an updated GST calculator for the latest rates.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is GST charged on basic groceries?**
A: Most unprocessed groceries (rice, wheat, fresh vegetables, milk) are GST-exempt (0%). Packaged and processed versions carry 5% or 12% GST.

**Q: Can I claim GST refund as a consumer?**
A: Generally, no. Only registered businesses can claim ITC. However, tourists (foreign nationals) can claim IGST refunds on certain goods purchased in India.

**Q: What is the penalty for not paying GST?**
A: Businesses that evade GST face penalties of 10% of tax due (or ₹10,000, whichever is higher). In cases of fraud, the penalty can be 100% of the evaded tax plus imprisonment.

- Essential items at 0–5%; luxury goods at 28%
